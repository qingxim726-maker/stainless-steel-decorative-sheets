# Stainless Steel Decorative Sheets Manufacturer

Foshan Jasun Import and Export Co., Ltd. is a professional supplier of stainless steel sheets, coils, strips, discs and decorative stainless steel products in Foshan, China.

## Main Products

- PVD Colored Stainless Steel Sheets
- 8K Mirror Stainless Steel Sheets
- Hairline and No.4 Finish
- Sandblasted and Etched Sheets
- Embossed Stainless Steel Sheets
- Water Ripple Stainless Steel
- Stainless Steel Coils, Strips and Discs
- Customized Sizes and Surface Finishes

## Available Materials

201 / 304 / 316 / 410 / 430 stainless steel

- Strict quality inspection for every sheet
